# Interview Minutes 11 – KVA Pohony s.r.o. (Plant Plzeň)
**Interviewee:** Elena Nováková (Plant Manager), Petr Dvořák (Finance & Logistics, joined after 20 min) · **Date:** 30.01.2026 · **Format:** MS Teams, English · **Interviewer:** L. Hartmann
*Minutes in English as conducted. German summary at the end.*

---

## Situation in Plzeň
- 118 employees (incl. 14 temporary workers), 2 shifts, plan for a 3rd assembly line in 2027.
- ERP: Dynamics NAV 2013 R2, customised heavily by local partner „DataSoft Plzeň". Partner announced end of cooperation by **31.12.2026**.
- Server hardware from 2016, no failover. "If the server dies, we are back to paper."
- 31 NAV users today; "with the new line we will need around 50."

## Key Requirements
1. **Czech localisation** – VAT control statement (kontrolní hlášení), Intrastat, Czech accounting standards (statutory), e-invoicing as per Czech rules, Czech payroll interface (payroll stays with local provider).
2. **User interface in Czech** for shop floor and warehouse. "Our operators don't speak German and only some speak English." (Note LH: Brenner said in kickoff "English is enough".)
3. **Intercompany** – deliveries Plzeň → Singen as automatic purchase/sales orders; transfer pricing; today all by Excel and e-mail.
4. **Visibility** – Singen planning should see Plzeň capacity and stock, and vice versa.
5. Quality: Same traceability as Singen (castings are machined in Plzeň!).
6. Local support partner in Czech Republic with Czech-speaking consultants.

## Timing
- "We cannot wait until 2029." Request: Plzeň **in phase 1** or an interim solution.
- Petr Dvořák suggests a "quick" separate cloud ERP for Plzeň as a bridge (6 months), then migration later. Elena is sceptical: "Two migrations in three years? No."

## Opinions
- Prefers cloud: "We have no IT department. One external guy, four hours a week."
- Concerned that Singen will "design everything for Germany and we have to adapt".
- Mentions that a sister company of a local customer uses a Czech ERP that is "very cheap and good for local requirements", but would not recommend for group.
